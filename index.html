What cleaning services does Clearview offer in Calgary?

Window cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, soft washing, high dusting and greenhouse cleaning for residential and commercial properties in Calgary and nearby communities.

How much does window cleaning cost in Calgary?

Residential window cleaning starts at $150. The final price depends on the size of your home, the number of windows and how accessible they are. Package discounts apply when you combine it with gutter cleaning or pressure washing.

What areas does Clearview Cleaning serve?

Calgary and the surrounding area, including Airdrie, Cochrane, Chestermere, Okotoks and Diamond Valley. Contact us to confirm availability in your community.

How long has Clearview Cleaning been in business?

We've been serving the Calgary area since 1988, with over 35 years of experience.
